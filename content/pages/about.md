My name is Michael Burch. I build software and infrastructure across cloud computing, local AI, and privacy-focused tooling, out of a home office in Round Rock, Texas.

I'm drawn to open source and local AI because they give you more control and fewer dependencies: running models on your own GPUs and NPUs, owning your authentication and communication, and not being locked into a single provider. I like taking complex systems and making them easier to deploy, operate, and use.

This blog is where I write about that work — cloud computing, containers, GPUs, hardware, and the lessons that come from building things.
**All content and opinions here are my own and do not represent my employer's view in any way.**

My participation in social media is deliberately limited, but I would enjoy hearing from you either through the comments on this blog or via an e-mail to [me@michaelburch.net](mailto:me@michaelburch.net?subject=%20hello%20from%20michaelburch.net)

## These days

I work at Microsoft as a Principal Software Engineer, designing and building agentic AI systems for Fortune 500 customers. Most of that work lives in private repos. On my own time, I'm exploring:

- Local and on-device AI across CPUs, GPUs, and NPUs ([oddo-desktop](https://github.com/oddohq/oddo-desktop))
- Generative AI runtimes and model optimization ([Lemonade](https://github.com/lemonade-sdk/lemonade))
- Cross-platform desktop applications ([Kava](https://github.com/michaelburch/kava))
- OIDC/OAuth2 and passkey authentication gateways ([Vouchrs](https://github.com/vouchrs/vouchrs))
- Rust, C#, C++, Python, and TypeScript

Away from the keyboard I [run](https://www.strava.com/athletes/michaelburch) and [hike](https://www.alltrails.com/members/michael-burch-9), mostly because this kind of work is very sedentary.

## Background

I spent a couple of decades getting here the long way.

I started out in the late 90s at an ISP owned by a telephone company, then spent years as a systems administrator across very different worlds: my personal favorite [The Vermont Country Store](https://www.vermontcountrystore.com/), oilfield manufacturing at [Halliburton](https://www.halliburton.com), pharma at Genzyme, and federal security compliance at [MIT Lincoln Laboratory](https://www.ll.mit.edu/about). In between, I deployed and ran VSAT satellite networks in [austere conditions](../images/austere.jpeg) in southern Afghanistan.

In 2015 I joined [Xamarin](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/apps/xamarin) just before the Microsoft acquisition, and moved from operations into engineering: multi-cloud automation and SaaS product development at RedPoint Global, cloud architecture leadership at IBM, and cloud-native consulting at Microsoft, where I built container and Kubernetes IP and modernized applications across finance, retail, manufacturing, and healthcare. Along the way I architected large-scale Azure and Kubernetes platforms for autonomous vehicles at General Motors.

When you've been the person responsible for building servers, racking, stacking, connecting and configuring them, an automated cloud deploy still feels like [a new Apple product](https://theoatmeal.com/comics/apple) showing up at the door, and you'll never want to go back.

## More

I was so captivated by the text-input graphic adventure game [Space Quest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Quest_I), that I learned to spell at an early age just to complete the game. My first coding experience was in BASIC, where I attempted to write a game that would become more popular than [Test Drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_Drive_(1987_video_game)). My ambition out-paced both my skill and the capabilities of my computer, but it sparked a passion for technology that has carried me a long way with very little formal education.

I've lived in Oklahoma, Texas, New York, Vermont, Massachusetts, and Afghanistan to name just a few places. I have [a loving wife and wonderful children](../images/family2019.jpeg), and I am motivated by anything that enables me to learn something new. I love being around other people who have a positive attitude.
